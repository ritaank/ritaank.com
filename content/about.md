I'm a Senior Systems Software Engineer at NVIDIA, where I work on self-driving technology, mostly on systems for foundation models in autonomous driving.

On [Alpamayo Platform](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/solutions/autonomous-vehicles/alpamayo/), I work on the platform layer underneath models like [Alpamayo R1](https://research.nvidia.com/publication/2025-10_alpamayo-r1): onboard deployment, runtime performance, stability, alignment, and integration with real vehicles.

Earlier at NVIDIA, I worked on [DRIVE IX](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/drive-ix-ecosystem/) and in-cabin AI platform work.

I graduated from MIT with degrees in Computer Science and Mathematics, and an M.Eng in AI. I think deeply about designing systems: how they perform, scale, fail, and feel to use.
